Terms & Conditions

RTB means Raising The Barre Ltd

RTB will not be held responsible for any injuries that may occur whilst performing at the Masterclass Weekend provided RTB have given a full 30 minute warm up session at the beginning of the day. It is the responsibility of the people taking part in the masterclass weekend to keep themselves fittingly warm at all times.

All persons taking part in the Masterclass weekend must be in good health and physically fit to take part. RTB have the right to exclude persons who appear not to be so.
All persons taking part must be drug and alcohol free at all times. If you are suspected to be under the influence of said alcohol and drugs by any members of RTB faculty and management you will be questioned and if suspected to be so by your ability or inability to walk/talk in a proper manor. In the unlikely event of this happening, you will be asked to leave and under those circumstances RTB will not repay the fee.

Refund Policies

The fee for the Masterclass weekend, will not be refunded unless the weekend is cancelled by RTB. If those circumstances occur, the fee will be refunded in full.
If a student has booked and paid for the Weekend Masterclass and becomes ill before the said weekend, and we are able to replace them with another student, we will repay the fee with minimal admin charges of £25. If we are unable to find a replacement student no fee can be refunded.
