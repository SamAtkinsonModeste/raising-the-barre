Our Mission Statement

I have been working professionally in the theatre since 1975 and, although my experience has covered most disciplines, my abiding interest has always been in musical theatre. I have appeared in musicals throughout the country, but mainly in The West End where I have worked with many of the finest Choreographers, Directors and Musical Directors in the business.

During the run of Kiss Me Kate at the Old Vic Theatre in 2011, Sir Trevor Nunn directing, and Steven Mere choreographing, I began to formulate an idea of organising professional Masterclasses given by the leading creatives with whom I have had the privilege to work. As I approached each individual, I discovered that they, like me, felt the word "Masterclass" seemed to have lost its true meaning, as these classes were rarely led by the true masters (people who actually create).

'Raising The Barre' sets out to make a dramatic change. 'RTB' Masterclasses will be given by the award winning master creators themselves. What we seek to do, is introduce you to working with these top people. They will help you attain that 'extra' something that lifts you above the 'good' performer. We want you to experience the hard physical work required. We want you to experience the joy of joining together as a company to create a small piece of your own theatrical history. We want you to leave at the end of the weekend exhausted, totally fulfilled, and having raised your own personal barre.

For me, being a part of that creative process has always been a wonderful and exciting privilege. Reaching the day when our rehearsals end and the performance begins is the most exhilarating part of the whole process. Everyone, as one, making the magic happen....'The Performance'.....There is no greater feeling than when the audience goes crazy as the curtain falls. And nothing more satisfying than knowing, you and your company have given the night your very best.

Master your Art... Never rest.....Be the best.

Paul Grunert
