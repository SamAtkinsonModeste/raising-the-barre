THE MASTERCLASS

It's The Day

So, you're here!

You are one of the lucky ones that have made it to the weekend. From now on its all hard work, sweat, fun and more sweat! On the first day we will all meet at the venue where the RTB weekend is to be held. All the details will be emailed to you, but generally it will be a 9am start time. We will have what is called a 'Meet and Greet'. This is the time when we introduce ourselves to you, and you, introduce us to you, as well as each other! The Choreographers and the Musical Director will inform you of what they will expect from you throughout the RTB weekend. After some 'getting to know each other' games you will be split into groups. This may be done through ages rather than abilities. We will expect all of you to be of a high standard. During the classes you will be learning some of the more complicated steps that the choreographer will use in their routine. No doubt you will get a coffee break during the morning at some point! You will also get a lunch hour. I am expecting a 9 to 10 hour day, so you won't be finishing much before 7pm.

THE MASTERCLASS

Second Day

At the end of the second day, you will be required to perform the routines as though it was opening night at the Theatre. It is bound to be a tough, tiring and exhilarating time, but we hope you will leave having made new friends and contacts, but most of all with a spring in your step knowing that you have learned a little something extra that will be of great use to you in the professional theatre.
If it is possible, your teachers and parents will be allowed to come and watch the final routine. (Depending on studio size). It will be filmed and photographs will be taken so that you have the possibility of keeping a small record of your time with us.
See you there!
