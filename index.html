RAISING THE BARRE

MUSICAL THEATRE MASTERCLASS

Bringing Them To You!

RTB Presents a new and exciting series of Musical Theatre Masterclasses. They will be given by Award Winning And leading Choreographers and Musical Directors From London's West End and New York's Broadway! We are hoping (availability permitting) to produce twelve a year. So, one each month in a City near you! There are only a limited number of places. The Masterclasses will take place over a weekend. This is a once in a lifetime experience to work alongside these extraordinary creators in a fully professional environment.

THE EXPERIENCE

The experience has been designed for you to get a real taste of what to expect when working at the top end of Musical Theatre. It's Fast, it's Hard, it's Exhausting and it's Exhilarating!
You could be working alongside Choreographers such as Chet Walker, Stephen Mear, Jack Samuel Gill, and Andrew Wright.
Musical Director such as Gareth Valentine, Nicholas Skilbeck, Stephen Ridley, Robert Scott, Jae Alexander and Nick Finlow.
Each one of these people have their own style and way of working. All of them have worked and created in either London's West End. Or America's Broadway. As well as Films and music videos. They are at the pinnacle of their profession! They are always on the look out for new and outstanding talent in both song and dance. So, bearing all of this in mind this could be a step for you towards working for them in the professional theatre.
So don't miss out on this opportunity and whatever you do, don't miss out on ‘THE EXPERIENCE‘!
